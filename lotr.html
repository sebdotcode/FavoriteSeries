The Lord of the Rings

The Lord of the Rings is one of my all time favorite movie series. I first watched them when I was 11 or 12 years old and I've been a fan ever since. I read the books as well (including The Hobbit) and I liked them although the movies are my personal favorite. The books obviously have a lot more lore explained, however I think the movies are a better starting point for people who aren't familiar or used to Tolkien's world. I'll also give the Hobbit movies an honorable mention.
