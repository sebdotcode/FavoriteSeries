Star Wars

George Lucas

George Lucas created one of the most beloved works of fiction ever made. Multiple generations of human beings are fans of Star Wars and it defined a new era of science fiction. My personal favorite would have to be The Original Trilogy although I also love the Prequels. I believe the Prequel's flaws are part of their charm.

Disney

After aquiring the Star Wars IP for $ billion dollars, Disney set out to continue the Star Wars universe. If Disney did it right, that's a completely different debate but more Star Wars is always a good thing.
