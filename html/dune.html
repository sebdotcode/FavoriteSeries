Dune

There are many groups and characters in Dune although my personal favorite would have to be the Spacing Guild. They are a secretive group who reveres mathematics and are the only facilitators of interstellar travel in the Dune universe. The Spacing Guild are addicted to spice and require it as payment in exchange for their services. The most esteemed position in the Spacing Guild is to be a Guild Navigator.
